## Third-generation low-orbit broadband model

The deployed model uses original geometry and procedural textures. The published design specifies four 19 m blanket segments per solar array, four backhaul antennas and six optical terminals. The model places the four strips side by side in each wing. The forked supports, two rows of three antenna faces and four outboard dishes follow the published illustrations. [SpaceX design overview](https://starlink.com/updates/starlink-version-3-satellites) · [SpaceX 2025 progress report, printed page 71](https://starlink.com/public-files/starlinkProgressReport_2025.pdf#page=72) · [Illustrated comparison of three generations](https://i.pcmag.com/imagery/articles/06KUFmNndIfHJBXmv8yIRMV-1.fit_lim.v1760468750.png)

The estimated bus measures 5.8 m long, 2.6 m wide and 0.30 m thick. Each wing is approximately 2.85 m wide and begins 2.35 m from the bus, giving a modeled tip-to-tip span of 45.3 m. Dish diameter (1.04 m), pointing angles, terminal placement, reverse-side equipment and surface finishes are visual estimates. The GLB also records these assumptions and references in its asset metadata.

[JMA technical report](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/mscweb/technotes/msctechrep-sp_201603.pdf) · [ESA imager and sounder](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2022/09/MTG-I_and_MTG-S) · [NASA relay generations](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20180007428/downloads/20180007428.pdf#page=13)

Run `python tools/build_orbit_models.py` to rebuild the generated fleet meshes, model manifest and compact NORAD index. The generator reuses existing spacecraft assets, including the shared broadband models. To refresh LEO or GEO platform assignments, run `python tools/import_orbit_profiles.py leo satcat.tsv satcat100k.tsv` or use `geo` with the corresponding GCAT snapshot before rebuilding.

The additional GEO exteriors are original procedural meshes. The design records in `tools/geo_appearances.json` and `tools/geo_fleet.json` identify the reference spacecraft and sources. Gunter Dirk Krebs archives many of the cited illustrations. The viewer labels profiles based on catalog outlines. [Gunter’s Space Page](https://space.skyrocket.de/) · [GCAT](https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/index.html)
